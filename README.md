
# VERITY
### The Trust Infrastructure for Every Brand.

**VERITY** is an AI-powered Brand Integrity and Governance Intelligence Platform that helps organisations evaluate marketing content, verify factual claims and maintain brand consistency.

## Problem

Organisations publish content across advertisements, websites, social media and marketing documents. Inconsistent messaging, unsupported claims and policy violations can damage brand credibility.

## Solution

VERITY provides an integrated intelligence platform that analyses campaign content against brand guidelines, evaluates factual claims using evidence and supports human governance decisions.

## Core Capabilities

1. Brand Voice Intelligence
2. Governance Intelligence
3. Evidence Intelligence
4. Document and Visual Intelligence
5. Human Review and Approval
6. Trust Graph and Audit Intelligence

## Core Workflow

UNDERSTAND → ANALYSE → VERIFY → GOVERN
